Phuket — The Starting Point

Opening Nights  ·  Southern Thailand
Beachfront ★ Great Start

Beachfront Boutique Hotel

The Tide Beachfront Hotel

📍 Phuket, Southern Thailand

A polished, well-positioned beachfront property to ease into the trip. Phuket can feel overwhelming — the right hotel insulates you from the chaos and gives you a clean, calm base. The Tide does exactly this: thoughtful design, direct beach access, and staff who seem genuinely interested in making the stay work. A proper opening chapter before the quieter islands begin.

Direct beachfront access — no crossing roads Good balance of design and value Well-positioned for Phuket's best beaches Organised transfers to ferry piers for onward travel
💰 Mid-range
★★★★☆ 8.3/10

Phuket Tips

Navigating Phuket

Patong has nightlife; Kata and Kamala are calmer — choose your base accordingly Ferry connections to Koh Lanta run from Rassada and Ao Nang piers Avoid peak season (Dec–Feb) — prices spike and crowds are intense Rent a scooter cautiously — roads are dangerous

Koh Lanta — The Transition Island

Phuket → Koh Lanta  ·  Trang Province
Island Resort

Beachside Resort

Moonlight Bay Resort

📍 Koh Lanta, Krabi Province

Koh Lanta hits a sweet spot that fewer travellers find — big enough to have good restaurants and bikes to rent, small enough to feel genuinely unhurried. Moonlight Bay Resort is a relaxed property positioned well on the island's western coast. Sunsets here are serious, and the resort is honest about what it is: a comfortable, easygoing base rather than anything showpiece. We used it as a launching point for long days on the island and the ferry south.

West coast position — made for sunset watching Comfortable, low-key — no pretension Good access to Koh Lanta's best beaches Ferry connections onward to Koh Mook
💰 Budget to Mid-range
★★★★☆ 7.9/10

Also Stopped Here

Nature Hill Resort

📍 Koh Lanta → Koh Mook transit stop

A transit night between islands — practical rather than memorable. Clean, well-run, and served the purpose perfectly. The kind of stay that doesn't need to be remarkable to be exactly right.

Koh Mook — The Quiet One

Koh Mook  ·  Trang Province
Boutique Island ★ Hidden Gem

Island Boutique Resort

Mook Montra Resort

📍 Koh Mook, Trang Province

Koh Mook is Thailand's best-kept secret in the Trang archipelago. Almost no package tourists, real fishing village atmosphere, and the Emerald Cave — a sea cave you swim through in darkness to emerge in a hidden lagoon — a short long-tail away. Mook Montra Resort is perfectly calibrated for the island: a beautiful, quiet, genuinely off-grid feeling property. No glossy overproduction. Just good design, clean rooms, and the ocean.

Genuinely quiet island — low tourist footprint 5 minutes by long-tail to Emerald Cave Beautiful, understated design Real fishing village adjacent — authentic Thailand
💰 Mid-range
★★★★★ 9.0/10

Koh Mook Don't-Miss

The Emerald Cave

Tham Morakot is reached by swimming 80 metres through a sea cave in complete darkness — then emerging into a sunlit hidden lagoon with white sand beach and sheer karst walls overhead. Go early (before 10am) before day-trip boats arrive. A snorkel and some nerve is all you need.

Best at low tide — more cave to swim through Long-tail from the resort is 5–10 minutes Avoid weekends and peak season

Koh Kradan & Bangkok — Finale

Koh Kradan → Trang → Bangkok
Island Bungalow ★ Trip Highlight

Beachfront Bungalow Resort

Reef Resort

📍 Koh Kradan, Trang Province

The final island, and the one that stayed with us longest. Koh Kradan has no roads, no ATMs, and very few visitors — which means the coral directly off the beach is extraordinary. Reef Resort places you on the sand — bungalows literally on the beach — and the snorkelling from your doorstep rivals dedicated dive sites. Sunsets from the west-facing beach are cinematic. This is the kind of place you extend your stay at without hesitation.

Beachfront bungalows — sand at the door Outstanding house reef — no boat needed to snorkel No roads — complete quiet and darkness at night West-facing: the best sunsets of the entire trip Low visitor numbers — stays genuinely wild
💰 Mid-range
★★★★★ 9.4/10

Bangkok Transit Night

Amari Don Muang Airport Hotel

📍 Don Mueang Airport, Bangkok

When the island trip ends and you're catching a morning flight, the Amari Don Muang is the correct answer. A skywalk connects directly to the terminal — you essentially live at the airport without the airport noise. Surprisingly good restaurant, proper beds, and the kind of transfer efficiency that makes island-hopping logistics manageable.

★★★★☆ 8.0/10 · Mid-range · Transit Night

Island Hop Logistics

Kradan → Trang by long-tail + minivan (arrange via resort) Trang to Bangkok by flight — AirAsia and Bangkok Airways both fly it Book island transfers at least a day ahead — limited capacity

The Island-Hop Blueprint

What the route
actually taught us.

The Trang archipelago — Koh Lanta, Koh Mook, Koh Kradan — is the under-the-radar version of a southern Thailand trip. We did this route just ahead of the crowds discovering it properly. Here's what made it work.

01

Go south of Krabi

Koh Phi Phi and Koh Samui are overrun. The Trang islands are the version of Thailand that still has some magic intact. For now.

02

Book transfers before you arrive

Inter-island connections are long-tail boats with limited space. Miss the scheduled departure and you wait a day. Your resort can book these — do it on arrival.

03

Reef Resort is worth the extra night

Koh Kradan is the most beautiful island on the route. We'd have stayed two nights. The snorkelling is extraordinary and the sunsets earn their reputation.

04

End in Bangkok for the flight

Don Muang airport hotel is the right call. The skywalk connection and early check-in options mean you arrive rested. Worth the one night.
