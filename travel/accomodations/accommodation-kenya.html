Diani Beach — The Indian Ocean Start

Days 1 & 2  ·  31 Aug – 1 Sep 2023
All-Inclusive ★ Loved It Days 1 & 2

Swahili Beachfront Resort

Diani Sea Resort & Diani Sea Lodge

📍 Diani Beach, Coast Province, Kenya

Diani Beach was the revelation of this trip — we genuinely hadn't expected Kenya's coast to be this beautiful. The twin resorts (Sea Resort and Sea Lodge, operated by the same group) are all-inclusive and sit right on an almost absurdly white strip of beach facing the Indian Ocean. The Swahili architectural character is the real differentiator: carved wood, dhow-inspired arches, and the kind of considered design you only find when someone cares about place as much as product. Sundowners with the ocean turning gold remain among our favourite travel memories.

White sand beachfront — virtually no crowds All-inclusive includes meals, drinks and water sports Distinctive Swahili-inspired architecture Short flight from Nairobi — only ~1 hour Excellent base for Shimba Hills & Wasini Island day trips
💰 Mid-range (All-Inclusive) 🛌 2 nights
★★★★★ 9.1/10

Which to Choose?

Sea Resort vs Sea Lodge

Both are operated by the same group and share the beach. The itinerary listed either/or based on availability — the distinctions are subtle. The Resort tends to be slightly larger and more structured; the Lodge has a more intimate, boutique feel. If available, the Lodge is our preference. Both are excellent.

Practical Notes — Diani

Transfer from Ukunda airstrip — organised by resort All-inclusive includes select drinks Optional water sports (snorkelling, kayaking) available Good wifi — useful for planning the safari leg

Masai Mara — In the Reserve

Days 3 & 4  ·  2–3 Sep 2023
Safari Lodge ★ Safari HQ Days 3 & 4

Safari Lodge — Full Board

Jambo Mara Lodge

📍 Masai Mara National Reserve, Narok County

This is where the trip becomes unforgettable. Jambo Mara Lodge sits within the reserve — not near it, inside it. Your night sounds include the distant calls of hyena and the occasional proximity of elephants. The lodge is full-board, which makes complete sense here: you're far from any town, and the food is genuinely good. Day 4 was a full-day game drive tracking the Big 5 with a picnic lunch served under a lone tree at the heart of the reserve. The optional hot air balloon ride at $390/person is one of the genuinely great bucket-list experiences and worth the cost.

Inside the Masai Mara — wildlife outside your tent Full-board included — critical in remote reserves 4x4 game drives with private English-speaking guide Optional hot air balloon safari ($390/person — worth it) Night bonfires available after dinner
💰 Mid-range (Full Board) 🛌 2 nights

En Route — Lake Naivasha

Crescent Island Stop

📍 Lake Naivasha, Great Rift Valley

The Day 3 journey from Nairobi to Masai Mara stops at Lake Naivasha — the only freshwater lake in the Great Rift Valley. Boat rides and a walking safari at Crescent Island (only accessible by boat) with giraffes, antelopes, hippos and birdlife. Optional at $50/person but genuinely worth it.

Masai Mara Tips

Book balloon safari the day before — pre-booking essential Morning drives (6–9am) are best for big cat sightings Wildebeest migration peaks Jul–Oct — plan accordingly Pack warm layers — mornings at altitude are cold

Lake Nakuru — The Flamingo National Park

Day 5  ·  4 Sep 2023  ·  Return to Nairobi Day 6
Lake Lodge Day 5

National Park Lodge

Lake Nakuru Lodge

📍 Lake Nakuru National Park, Nakuru County

The final night of the safari, and a different kind of beauty: Lake Nakuru is famous for its flamingo populations (the lake turns pink at scale) and is home to rhinos, lions, and leopards alongside the flamingos. The lodge sits within the park boundary and the arrival itself — a game drive along the lake's edge — serves as a soft re-entry to what's been a remarkable five days. The morning departure allows for an early game drive before the road back to Nairobi.

Inside Lake Nakuru National Park — lions and rhinos nearby Famous flamingo lake — spectacular at scale Arrival includes an evening game drive to the lodge Option for early morning drive before Nairobi departure

Day 5 Highlight — En Route

Masai Village Cultural Tour

The morning of Day 5 included a visit to a Masai village before leaving the Mara — one of the more genuinely moving parts of the trip. Dances, customs, traditions. Not performative in the way many such stops are; this one felt real.

Planning Your Kenya Safari

6 days.
What we'd do differently.

We went with Msanii Africa Safaris — tour leader Titus Mutua — and the operation was exceptional. Flawless logistics, great guide, genuine care throughout. For a trip of this nature with this many moving parts, using a specialist operator was the right call, without question.

01

Deposit 30% at least 3 months out

Msanii requires 30% deposit to confirm. Lodge capacity in Masai Mara is finite — don't leave this late. Online payment has no surcharge; card on arrival adds 3.5%.

02

Do the balloon safari

$390/person sounds steep. It is. But an hour over the Mara at dawn, landing for a champagne bush breakfast — this is one of the great experiences on earth. Requires pre-booking; briefing the day before.

03

Start at Diani — reverse the order

We went beach first, safari second. This works brilliantly — you arrive relaxed and ready, rather than arriving at a beach having just driven through the bush.

04

Full board matters in remote lodges

There are no restaurants outside the gate. Always choose full board for Mara and Nakuru — it's not just convenience, it's the only sensible option.
