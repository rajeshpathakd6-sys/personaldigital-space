Practical Tips for Taiwan

🛵
Getting Around

The High Speed Rail links the west coast fast; for the east and the mountains, rent a car or scooter. EasyCard works on almost all public transport.

🍜
Eat at Markets

Night markets are the heart of Taiwanese food culture. Go hungry, carry cash, and follow the longest local queues.

⛈️
Avoid Typhoons

Typhoon season runs Jul–Sep. Travelling Oct–Apr means cooler weather and far fewer washed-out plans.
