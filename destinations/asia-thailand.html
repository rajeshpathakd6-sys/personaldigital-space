Practical Tips for Thailand

🛺
Getting Around

Grab (ride-hailing) is cheap and removes the haggling. For islands, book the boat+transfer combos — they’re smoother than arranging legs separately.

🙏
Temple Dress

Cover shoulders and knees at temples and the Grand Palace. Carry a light scarf; some sites turn you away otherwise.

🌧️
Two Coasts, Two Seasons

The Andaman (west) and Gulf (east) coasts have opposite rainy seasons. Pick your islands to match the month.
