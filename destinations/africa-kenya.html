Practical Tips for Kenya

🦓
Time the Migration

River crossings in the Maasai Mara peak Jul–Oct. Book camps early — the good ones fill a year ahead for migration season.

💉
Health Prep

Yellow-fever vaccination may be required, and malaria prophylaxis is recommended for the parks and coast. Carry repellent with DEET.

📱
Pay by Phone

M-Pesa mobile money is everywhere. A local Safaricom SIM makes payments, data and matatu travel far easier.
