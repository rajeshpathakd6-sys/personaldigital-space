Practical Tips for Sri Lanka

🚂
Book the Train Early

The Kandy–Ella observation car sells out weeks ahead in season. Book via 12go.asia or Sri Lanka Railways; otherwise grab a 2nd-class reserved seat.

🛺
Hire a Driver

Distances look short but roads are slow. A driver-guide for a few days is affordable and turns transit into part of the trip.

🌊
Two Coasts, Two Seasons

The west and south are best Dec–Mar; the east (Nilaveli, Arugam Bay) shines May–Sep. Plan your beaches around the monsoon.
