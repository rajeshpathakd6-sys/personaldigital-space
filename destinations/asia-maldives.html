Practical Tips for Maldives

💸
Local Islands Save Money

Guesthouses on local islands (Maafushi, Thulusdhoo) cost a fraction of resorts. Public ferries and speedboats connect them to Malé.

🍶
Know the Rules

Local islands are alcohol-free and modest dress applies away from tourist beaches. Resorts and liveaboards are the exception.

🌊
Pick Your Season

Nov–Apr is dry and calm; May–Oct is wetter but cheaper and great for surf. Visibility is best in the dry months.
